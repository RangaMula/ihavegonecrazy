# Jogajog: 100 software directories and marketplaces for SEO and credibility (Nov 2026)

**Priority:** **A** = do in the first two weeks after launch · **B** = within 1–2 months · **C** = later / only if relevant.
**Ground rules:** use one consistent name, description, logo and URL (jogajog.app) everywhere; ask real clients for reviews and never buy reviews; most sites ban incentivised reviews unless disclosed. Fees and policies change, so check each site.
**Suggested categories:** Membership Management Software · Association Management Software (AMS) · Alumni Management · Member Directory · Nonprofit / Club Management · Communication (notices).
