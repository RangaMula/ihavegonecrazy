# Global startup funding directory (Nov 2026)

Independent of any one startup. Five lists of 25: angel investor platforms, VC platforms, funds similar to Conjunction Capital, government-sponsored accelerators, and university-attached accelerators.

**Src column:** **V** = checked in web searches on 8 Nov 2026 · **K** = from general knowledge; confirm dates, fees and eligibility on the official site before applying. Deadlines and terms change often.
**Contact:** the official website and its application form are the primary channel for every entry; no email addresses are guessed. For funds, a warm introduction from a portfolio founder works best.
