# MISA licence fee status and cost of establishing a business in Saudi Arabia

Research date: 19 September 2026
Exchange rate used: SAR 3.75 = USD 1 (fixed peg)

## 1. Is the MISA licence free now?

Short answer: the MISA government fee is currently zero for the establishment phase, but "free" is a fee suspension and a policy shift, not a permanent legal exemption.

What changed:

- The new Investment Law (issued August 2024, in force 12 February 2025) repealed the Foreign Investment Law of 2000. It replaced the old "foreign investment licence" with **registration** in a National Investor Register run by MISA. Foreign investors still must register with MISA before starting activity, but registration is a notification-style step rather than a discretionary licence.
- Under the old regime MISA charged SAR 2,000 per year for licence issuance plus an annual "subscription" of SAR 10,000 in year one and SAR 60,000 in later years for a standard services licence (often quoted as SAR 12,000 then SAR 62,000). Entrepreneur licences were as low as SAR 2,000 per year.
- Multiple Saudi setup advisers report that MISA suspended those fees as part of the investor-facilitation package and that the suspension has run for roughly two years through 2026. MISA's own Investor Guide no longer prints a tariff. It says the investor pays "the fee for the approved registration period as determined by MISA" within 15 business days of approval.

Practical reading:

- Budget SAR 0 for the MISA registration fee, but keep a contingency of up to SAR 12,000 in case the suspension ends or MISA sets a fee at approval.
- Confirm the live invoice on the MISA e-services portal at the time of filing. The primary MISA sources (misa.gov.sa FAQ and Investor Guide PDF) could not be fetched from this environment, so the "currently suspended" finding rests on several independent adviser reports rather than an official announcement quoted verbatim.
- Consultant and PRO fees are not affected by the waiver.

## 2. Cost of establishing a business (100% foreign-owned LLC)

Base scenario: a services LLC in Riyadh, 100% foreign owned, one expatriate general manager on an iqama, no Saudi employees in year one.

### 2.1 One-time setup costs

| Item | SAR | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MISA investment registration | 0 (contingency up to 12,000) | Fee suspended; historically 2,000 + 10,000 first-year subscription |
| Commercial Registration, Chamber of Commerce, municipality (unified invoice via Saudi Business Center) | 1,800 – 2,100 | CR 1,200 + e-publishing 500 + chamber tier; larger firms up to 5,000 chamber |
| Articles of Association drafting, notarisation, publication | 1,000 – 3,000 | Often bundled by the consultant |
| Legalisation and Arabic translation of parent documents (CR, AoA, financials, board resolution, POA) | 3,000 – 8,000 | Saudi embassy + MOFA attestation |
| National address (Saudi Post) | 0 – 500 | Mandatory for CR, bank, visas |
| Qiwa, Muqeem, Absher, GOSI portal activation | 2,000 – 3,000 | Labour and residency systems |
| GM work visa and iqama issuance (visa, medical, insurance, iqama card) | 8,000 – 12,000 | Excludes the annual levy below |
| Corporate bank account opening | 0 – 2,000 | 2 to 4 weeks; in-person KYC |
| VAT / ZATCA registration | 0 | Mandatory once taxable turnover exceeds SAR 375,000 |
| Consultant / PRO package | 15,000 – 40,000 | Packages advertised "from USD 5,500" (about SAR 20,600); check what is excluded |
| **Subtotal one-time** | **31,000 – 83,000** | |

### 2.3 Capital

Capital is money deposited into the company's own account, not a fee, but it must be available.

- Services or consulting LLC: no statutory minimum under the Companies Law. MISA and banks commonly expect SAR 100,000 to 500,000 to be shown as paid-up capital.
- Entrepreneur licence (startups with an accredited incubator or accelerator letter): zero capital requirement.
- Trading (wholesale and retail) with 100% foreign ownership: SAR 30 million minimum capital, presence in three or more countries.
- Industrial licence: capital set by project; work permit levy permanently waived since December 2025.

### 2.4 Year-one totals

| Scenario | SAR | USD |
|---|---|---|
| Lean: entrepreneur or services registration, virtual office, one GM, light consultant support | 45,000 – 65,000 | 12,000 – 17,000 |
| Typical: services LLC, full consultant package, serviced office, one GM | 70,000 – 120,000 | 19,000 – 32,000 |
| Established: small fitted office, one GM plus one Saudi hire, full compliance | 150,000 – 250,000 | 40,000 – 67,000 |

These ranges exclude paid-up capital, salaries other than the one Saudi hire noted, and any sector licences (health, education, finance, construction classification) that carry their own fees.

### 2.5 Costs that appear from year two or at growth

- Saudization (Nitaqat) applies from five employees. A Saudi hire at the SAR 4,000 monthly floor plus GOSI costs roughly SAR 55,000 to 60,000 per year.
- GOSI: about 2% for expat staff (occupational hazards) and about 21.5% combined for Saudi staff, of which the employer pays 11.75%.
- Corporate income tax: 20% on the profit share attributable to foreign ownership. Zakat applies to any Saudi-owned share.
- If MISA reinstates its subscription fee, the old schedule was SAR 60,000 per year for a standard services licence.

## 3. Timeline

- MISA registration: 3 to 10 working days once documents are attested.
- Commercial Registration, AoA, chamber, municipality: 1 to 2 weeks.
- Bank account: 2 to 4 weeks.
- GM visa and iqama: 2 to 4 weeks after CR.
- Realistic end to end: 6 to 10 weeks. Document attestation abroad is the usual bottleneck.

## 4. Caveats

- MISA's official pages were not reachable from this environment. All fee-suspension statements come from Saudi setup consultancies published in 2025 and 2026 and should be confirmed on the MISA portal before relying on a zero figure.
- Adviser prices vary widely. Ask for an itemised written quote and check that iqama, levy, VAT setup, municipal licence, translation and Ejar office are either included or listed as exclusions.
- Chamber of Commerce tiers, municipal licence fees and office rent vary by city and activity.
