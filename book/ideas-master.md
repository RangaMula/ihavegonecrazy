# Master list of 103 SaaS ideas (source for the book)

Each idea: number, title, pitch, Bangladesh wedge, port countries, moat, indicative pricing. Prices are BDT (৳) unless stated. Keep these facts when writing chapters.

## 1. Local Business AI Presence
Pitch: audits and auto-manages a business's Google Business Profile (review replies, weekly posts, Q&A), generates an AEO-ready one-page website from the listing, reports rank on Google Maps and mentions in AI answers. Wedge: millions of local businesses with unanswered reviews and no website; nobody sells "AI search visibility" to a pharmacy in Barishal. Ports: global, any language; agencies white-label it. Moat: audit data, rank history, agency relationships, site hosting. Pricing: Free audit; Starter ৳499/month ($19); Pro ৳1,499 ($49); Agency ৳4,999 ($149). Growth loop: scrape Google Maps by city and category, pre-build a private audit page per business, personalised email or WhatsApp "claim your page", programmatic city-category pages.

## 2. WhatsApp AI Receptionist and Booking
Pitch: AI answers customer enquiries on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram in Bangla or English, books appointments, sends reminders, collects reviews for clinics, salons, restaurants, tutors. Wedge: WhatsApp and Messenger dominate customer contact in Bangladesh; owners miss messages. Ports: global via WhatsApp Business API. Moat: booking workflow, integrations, conversation history. Pricing: ৳999 to ৳2,999/month ($29 to $79). Cost note: Meta per-conversation fees and business verification.
