## Decisions

- Language list = `series-standard/languages/master_languages.csv` (festive-goodall branch, 143) + Bengali → `data/languages.csv`.
- Arabic = the Yaḥyā al-Laythī recension, sunnah.com text (hadith-json v1.2.0; 1,860 entries, 61 books); grades from
  fawazahmed0/hadith-api (Salim al-Hilali). Two source typos fixed in `data/arabic/corrections.json`.
- Third-party translations are used for research only, never copied.
- Pronunciation is generated by rule (`engine/`); the Bengali notation is the one in `books/ar/STYLE.md`.
- Meaning: the chain as a line of names + a full translation. Mālik's commentary in its own block. Fixed glossary for his formulas.
- Pilot = Book 1 (31 entries) in Bengali + Japanese. An independent AI review found 2 medium + 6 low issues; all fixed.
- Publishing:
  - 6 × 9 in paperback, EPUB 3.
  - Publisher Maktabatu Kunujul Akhirah (imprint of the Kunujul Akhirah Charitable Trust; Dhaka address and
    kunujulakhirah.org, as in the Arabic book).
  - © 2026 Maktabatu Kunujul Akhirah, all rights reserved. Editor: Hafez Abdullah Muhammad Minhaz Reza.
  - The copyright page states that the translation was made with AI assistance (KDP requires disclosure).
  - The contact email is left out of the books (`contact_email: null`); add it if wanted.
- The REVIEW DRAFT notice stays until `reviewed_by` is set (the series' own rule: scholar check before publishing).
