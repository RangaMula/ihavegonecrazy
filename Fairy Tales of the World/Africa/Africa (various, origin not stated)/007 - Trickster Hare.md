Hare met Elephant carrying a pot of honey.

Elephant carried Hare on his back. Hare ate Elephant’s honey.

They found a big buffalo drinking water in a river.

Hare wanted to know who was stronger, Buffalo or Elephant.

Hare said he had an idea how to find out.

Hare tied one end of a rope around Elephant’s waist.
