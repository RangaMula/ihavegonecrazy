Titi is a beautiful girl.

She lives in Opobo village.

Titi lives with her grandmother.

She has been longing to visit her parents.

Grandmother decided to take her to Bausa. 

Titi was happy to see her family and friends.

One day, Grandmother sent Titi to the market.

She gave her some money.

On her way to the market, Titi saw Donkey, the trickster.

Donkey saw Titi with money and asked her to clap her hands.

 Titi did so and the money fell down.

Donkey, the trickster, quickly took the money and ran away.

Titi called for help. 

People helped her to get back the money from Donkey.
