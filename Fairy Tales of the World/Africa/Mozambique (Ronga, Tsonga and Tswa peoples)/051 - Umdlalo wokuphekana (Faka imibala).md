# Umdlalo wokuphekana (Faka imibala)

- **Tradition:** Mozambique (Ronga, Tsonga and Tswa peoples) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *African Storybook — traditional tales retold (CC BY 4.0)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Mozambican folktale; Lunathi Ntunzi Reginaldo Vicente Manhice (story, illustration and translation credits as listed by African Storybook)
- **Original language of this text:** isiXhosa

Kudala-dala, umvundla nenkawu babengabahlobo. Ngenye imini, ngexesha lendlala, umvundla wamema umhlobo wakhe ukuba bazodlala.

Umvundla wathi, "Masidlale ukuphekaphekana!" Inkawu yabuza, "Sizakuyenza njani lonto?"

Ke ngoku umvundla waphendula, "Kufuneka sibase umlilo."

Sigcwalise imbiza ngamanzi, ke ngoku siyibeke emlilweni.

Mna ndiyakungena kuqala. Xa ndibetha isiciko sembiza, uvule. Ngoku ndizokuphuma.

Inkawu yavuma ukuyenza lonto. Ngoko umvundla wangena kuqala embizeni, amanzi eqala ukubila.

Umvundla wankqonkqoza, inkawu yasivula isiciko.

Kodwa kuthe xa ilithuba lenkawu, umvundla wathatha ilityekazi elikhulu, walibeka phezu kwesiciko sembiza esakugqiba wahlala phezu kwaso.

Kuthe xa amanzi eqala ukubila, inkawu yankqonkqoza, kodwa umvundla akakhange asivule isiciko.

Ngoku inkawu yafa, ke ngoku umvundla watya umhlobo wakhe!
