# Akidodok Nabworok

- **Tradition:** Mozambique (Ronga, Tsonga and Tswa peoples) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *African Storybook — traditional tales retold (CC BY 4.0)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Mozambican folktale; Lolupe teachers Hélder de Paz Alexandre (story, illustration and translation credits as listed by African Storybook)
- **Original language of this text:** Ng’aturkana
- **Source:** African Storybook story #13522 — https://www.africanstorybook.org/ (search the title)

Arai akidodok ka ayole ng'ipaalon.

Amalimali eleu lo ayole.

Abworok eleu lo akidodok.

Abu ayole tama napaekeng kitukulau ng'akipi.

Kipung'ar akidodok nawi akisub ng'una.

Dio akidodok kitukulau ng'akipi.

Tachakakin ng'esi nakipi na akulate.

Tonoma ng'akipi eleu keng kilebeleb.

Abu akidodok tobworokok noi!
