# Kungani sicoco sibe sibi

- **Tradition:** Mozambique (Ronga, Tsonga and Tswa peoples) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *African Storybook — traditional tales retold (CC BY 4.0)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Mozambican folktale Busile Ndhlovu Hélder de Paz Alexandre (story, illustration and translation credits as listed by African Storybook)
- **Original language of this text:** Siswati

Eminyekeni leminyenti leyengcile, umgololo nesicoco bewungebangani. Ngalelinye lilanga tancuma kuyobuka tintfombi edolobheni.

Sicoco besinemona nesikhumba semgololo lesicwebetelako nalesihle.

Satsi: Wente ngani kutsi kute ube muhle kangaka? Buka mine, Ngimubi kakhulu. Ngingenta njani ngesikhumba sami?

Umgololo watsi: Lalela lapha! Faka emanti ebhodweni bese uwabeka emmlilweni. Ngemva kwaloko ubese u.

Kepha sicoco satsi: Sengiyati, ngiyati. Ngicela usale ubindza mngani wami. Sicoco sase siyahamba.

Sitsite nasifika ekhaya safaka emanti ebhodweni. Sase siwafaka emlilweni.

Atsite emanti nasacala kubila, satiphosela ekhatsi ebhodweni.

Sikhumba saso sasha, umsila waso wabuye wajubeka.

Kun ekutsi sibe sihle njengemgololo, saba sibi kakhulu kundlula kucala!
