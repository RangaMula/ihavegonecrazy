# Manu ne Gyata no

- **Tradition:** Mozambique (Ronga, Tsonga and Tswa peoples) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *African Storybook — traditional tales retold (CC BY 4.0)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Mozambican folktale Edward Addo-Yobo Hélder de Paz Alexandre (story, illustration and translation credits as listed by African Storybook)
- **Original language of this text:** Asante Twi

Da bi, na Manu nantew.

Saa bere no, na gyata bi regye n'ahome wɔ sare no mu.

Ná ehu hyɛ Manu ma.

Gyata no nyanee.

Manu foroo dua tenten bi.

Gyata no twɛn Manu wɔ dua no ase.

Manu daeɛ.

Ɔhwee fam fii dua no so.

Gyata no dwane.

Manu fii ase tuu mmirika kɔɔ baabi a gyata no tuu mmirika.

Bere a ohui no, ɔsesae na odwane.
