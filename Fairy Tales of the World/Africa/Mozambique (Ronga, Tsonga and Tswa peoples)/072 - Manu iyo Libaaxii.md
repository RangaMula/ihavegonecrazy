# Manu iyo Libaaxii

- **Tradition:** Mozambique (Ronga, Tsonga and Tswa peoples) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *African Storybook — traditional tales retold (CC BY 4.0)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Mozambican folktale Abdullahi Mohamed Hélder de Paz Alexandre (story, illustration and translation credits as listed by African Storybook)

Maalin, ayaa Manu lugaynaayey.

Markaas, libaax ayaa ku nasanaayey cawska dhexdiisa.

Manu aad buu u cabsooday.

Libaaxii baa soo kacay.

Manu wuxuu fuulay geed dheer.

Libaaxii wuxuu Manu ku sugey geedka hoostiisa.

Manu wuu seexday.

Wuxuuna ka soo dhacay geedkii.

Libaaxii baa cararay.

Manu wuxuu bilaabay inuu u ordo dhinacii libaaxu u cararay.

Markuu isgartay, ayuu isbedeley oo dhinac kale u cararay.
