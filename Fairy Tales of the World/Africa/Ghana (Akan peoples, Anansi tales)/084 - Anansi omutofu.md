Yachama efiokhulia, halali shatekhangata.

"Khonye okhufuluka," Eshimuna shaboola. Anansi shiyenyata khulwa obutofu.

Abohanga obuusi khushilenje nende khunyungu eyeshimuna.

"Lishene, khulie fwesi amahenjera?" "Khukhonye okhukhola emilimo."

"Injiri, khulie amapwoni fwesi?" "Khonya okhukakasia," Injiri yaboola.

Mukhumalirikha, buli eshirenje sha Anansi shabohwa khunyungu.

Efilenje fiosi 8 fiakhweswa. "Leshe okhukhwesa!" Anansi yalira.

Neshichiranga tsimbubi tsiosi tsili nefilenje efinyere.
