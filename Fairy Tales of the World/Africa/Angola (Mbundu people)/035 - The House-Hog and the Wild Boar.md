# The House-Hog and the Wild Boar

- **Tradition:** Angola (Mbundu people) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *Folk-Tales of Angola (1894)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Chatelain, Héli (author, d. 1908)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Internet Archive item `folktalesofangol00chat` — https://archive.org/details/folktalesofangol00chat (OCR text, lightly cleaned)
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.
- **Note:** Digitised by OCR; minor recognition errors may remain.

---

Boar 663 used to be with his kinsman, Hog, in the forest. As they were, Hog said : “I am going to the village, to live with the men." Boar said : “ To the village, do not go there ; there they hate the animals.” Hog said : “ I will go to the village ; I shall always eat the food, that men eat ; in the bush there are bitter plants.”

Hog started ; he arrives in the village. They built him a sty ; he entered ; stayed. He bred in the village ; they seized him. Now they kill him ; because he has already left seed.

Whenever the hog squeaks, when they kill it, it is speaking, saying : “ Boar, he told me, saying i in the village, do not go there ; * I said, * to the same I will go.’ ”

When it is left alreadv with little life, it says : “ I die, I die, I, Hog.”

People, when they were, they had no hogs ; what brought the hogs to the habitations, (is) that the food, which the people are wont to eat, is good.
