# Partridge and Turtle

- **Tradition:** Angola (Mbundu people) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *Folk-Tales of Angola (1894)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Chatelain, Héli (author, d. 1908)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Internet Archive item `folktalesofangol00chat` — https://archive.org/details/folktalesofangol00chat (OCR text, lightly cleaned)
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.
- **Note:** Digitised by OCR; minor recognition errors may remain.

---

I will tell of Partridge who had a discussion with Turtle.

Partridge said: “Thou, friend Turtle, never canst run away. When the fire is coming into the land, thou art always burnt.” Turtle said : “ I cannot be burnt. Thou art burnt, thou, Partridge.” Partridge said : “ I have my wings ; I fly. Thou canst not fly, canst not run ; thou shalt burn just here, (in this) very same place.” They were silent.
