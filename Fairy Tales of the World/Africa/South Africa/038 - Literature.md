### FOOTNOTES

Black beeswax.

Wip : A Dutch word for springle, consisting of a bent green stick, to which a noose is attached at one end; the trap is delicately adjusted by a cross stick, which when trod on releases the bent bough, pulling the noose quickly around the animal and into the air.

(In token of approval, according to a Hottentot custom.)
