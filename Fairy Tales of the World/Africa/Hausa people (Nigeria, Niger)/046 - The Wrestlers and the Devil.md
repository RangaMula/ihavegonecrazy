# The Wrestlers and the Devil

- **Tradition:** Hausa people (Nigeria, Niger) (Africa)
- **Source book:** *Hausa Superstitions and Customs (1913)*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Tremearne, A. J. N. (author, d. 1915)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Internet Archive item `hausasuperstitio00tremuoft` — https://archive.org/details/hausasuperstitio00tremuoft (OCR text, lightly cleaned)
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.
- **Note:** Digitised by OCR; minor recognition errors may remain.

There lived once a Youth, Awudu, who was nicknamed the Strong One. His Father had 150 head of

Cattle, and he slaughtered them all, and made bags of the hides, and then he went off on a trading trip to sell them, and Awudu went out into the world to try his strength.

As he was travelling along, he met another Youth, called Hambari, and he also was noted for his strength ; he had just arrived at a well, and had opened his mouth and had drunk the water, when Awudu came upon him, and they travelled on together. Now they went on, and came to a running river, and Hambari beat the water with his hand, and the water divided into two. Then Awudu said " Hullo, Hambari, you certainly are strong."

As they were travelling they met Dashira who also was a powerful Man. And he said to them " Are you going out to try your strength?" And they said " Yes." He said that he would go also, and so there were now three of them.

As they were travelling, they met Tankoko who also was a powerful Man. And he said to them "Where are you going?" They replied "We are going out into the world to try our strength." And he said that he would accompany them, so there were now four of them.

Well, they went into the forest, and slept that night at the foot of a Monkey-bread tree, all four of them. Next morning they said " Ah ! the day has broken, let us go hunting, but let us leave Hambari to keep guard over our possessions." So they went off, the other three, to hunt.

Now, as it happened, there was a Devil in* the foot of the tree, and the Devil came out, and said " Hullo Hambari, it is reported that you are strong, get up and wrestle with me." So they got up and started wrestling,
