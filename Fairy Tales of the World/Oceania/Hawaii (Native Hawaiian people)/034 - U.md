# U

- **Tradition:** Hawaii (Native Hawaiian people) (Oceania)
- **Source book:** *The Legends and Myths of Hawaii: The fables and folk-lore of a strange people*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Kalakaua, David, King of Hawaii (author, d. 1891); Daggett, Rollin Mallory (editor, d. 1901)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #56597 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/56597
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.

---

Ua , a sea-bird; rain.

Uau , a large marine bird.

Uala , a potato.

Uila , lightning.

Uha , the thigh.

Uhi , a yam.

Ulu , the bread-fruit.

Ukeke , an ancient pulsatile musical instrument.

Ulili , a bamboo flute.

Uliuia , a beer made of cane-juice or the ti root.

Ulu-maika , a game of rolling round stone disks.

Ulaula , red; the sacred color.

Uliuli , blue.

Ulunu , a pillow or head-rest.

Unauna , a tabu mark.

Unihipili , the spirit of a deceased person.

Umiumi , the beard or whiskers.
