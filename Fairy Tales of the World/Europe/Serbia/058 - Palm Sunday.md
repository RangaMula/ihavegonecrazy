# Palm Sunday

- **Tradition:** Serbia (Europe)
- **Source book:** *Hero Tales and Legends of the Serbians*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Karadžić, Vuk Stefanović (author, d. 1864); Petrovitch, Woislav M. (author, d. 1934)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #38571 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/38571
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.

---

“In winter, just before Lent, the great festival in honour of the Dead is celebrated, at which every one solemnizes the memory of departed relations and friends, and no sooner does Palm Sunday arrive than the people join in commemorating the renovation of life.

“ On the preceding Saturday the maidens assemble on a hill, and recite poems on the resurrection of Lazarus; and on Sunday, before sunrise, they meet at the place where they draw water and dance their country dance ( kolllo ), chanting a song, which relates how the water becomes dull by the antlers of a stag, and bright by his eye.”
