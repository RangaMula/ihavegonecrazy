# An Ancient Charm Against The Fairies

- **Tradition:** Isle of Man (Manx people) (Europe)
- **Source book:** *Manx Fairy Tales*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Morrison, Sophia (author, d. 1917)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #51762 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/51762 (copied via the GITenberg mirror)
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.

---

Peace of God and peace of man,

Peace of God on Columb-Killey,

On each window and each door,

On every hole admitting moonlight,

On the four corners of the house,

On the place of my rest,

And peace of God on myself.

THE END
