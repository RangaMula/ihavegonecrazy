# Roberts And The Fairies

- **Tradition:** Wales (Europe)
- **Source book:** *Welsh Fairy-Tales and Other Stories*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Emerson, P. H. (Peter Henry) (editor, d. 1936)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #8675 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8675 (copied via the GITenberg mirror)
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.

Source: Told me by another old man, and I believe it to be genuine.

There is another story of the same kind, of a man who was searching for treasure in Beaumaris Castle, and after he had told of his luck a stone fell on him, so that he had to go away.
