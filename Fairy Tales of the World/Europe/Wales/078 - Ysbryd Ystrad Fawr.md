# Ysbryd Ystrad Fawr

- **Tradition:** Wales (Europe)
- **Source book:** *Welsh Folk-Lore
a Collection of the Folk-Tales and Legends of North Wales*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Owen, Elias (author, d. 1899)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #20096 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20096 (copied via the GITenberg mirror)
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.

“Yr oedd Ysbryd yn Ystrad Fawr, ger Llangwm, yn arfer ymddangos ar brydiau ar lun twrci, a’i gynffon o’i amgylch fel olwyn troell.  Bryd arall, byddai yn y coed, nes y byddai y rhai hyny yn ymddangos fel pe buasent oll ar dân; bryd arall, byddai fel ci du mawr yn cnoi asgwrn.”— Y Gordofigion , p. 106.

Ystrad Fawr Ghost in English is as follows:—

There was a Ghost at Ystrad Fawr, near Llangwm, that was in the habit of appearing like a turkey with his tail spread out like a spinning wheel.  At other times he appeared in the wood, when the trees would seem as if they were on fire, again he would assume the shape of a large black dog gnawing a bone.
