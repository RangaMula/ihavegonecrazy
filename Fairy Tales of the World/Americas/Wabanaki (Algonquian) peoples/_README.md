# Wabanaki (Algonquian) peoples

Continent: Americas  
Tales in this folder: **55** (fewer than 100: every distinct tale found in the public-domain sources below is included)

## Sources

- *Algonquin Legends of New England* — Leland, Charles Godfrey (author, d. 1903) — Gutenberg #6803 — 12 tales — PD worldwide
- *Algonquin Indian Tales* — Young, Egerton Ryerson (author, d. 1909) — Gutenberg #10891 — 11 tales — PD worldwide
- *In Indian Tents
Stories Told by Penobscot, Passamaquoddy and Micmac Indians to Abby L. Alger* — Alger, Abby Langdon (author, d. 1917) — Gutenberg #51908 — 23 tales — PD worldwide
- *Contribution to Passamaquoddy Folk-Lore* — Fewkes, Jesse Walter (author, d. 1930) — Gutenberg #17997 — 9 tales — PD worldwide
