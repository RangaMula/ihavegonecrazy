# Raven’S Dancing Blanket

- **Tradition:** Alaska Native peoples (Americas)
- **Source book:** *Myths and Legends of Alaska*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Judson, Katharine Berry (compiler, d. 1929)
- **Original language of this text:** en
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #47146 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/47146
- **Copyright status:** Public domain worldwide: the text was published long ago and every named author/translator died in or before 1955.

---

Tsimshian

O NE day Raven put on the shaman’s blanket of his grandfather. Then he went away; he strayed off. He was very poor and he tore his dancing blanket. Then he caught ravens. He used anything to kill the ravens. Then he took the skins of the ravens and tied them together. Then he walked about in them, dressed very well.

Now he saw a good shaman’s blanket like the one he had before. He tore his raven’s blanket. He took the dancing blanket that hung before him. Behold! it was not a shaman’s blanket. It was only the lichens on a tree. Now he saw it was only lichens. He sat down and wept. He took his old raven’s blanket and tied it together. Then once more he went on, weeping with hunger.
