- **Source book:** *Myths and Legends of Alaska*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Judson, Katharine Berry (compiler, d. 1929)
- **Original language of this text:** en

Athapascan ( Upper Yukon )

L ONG ago a hungry marten went to an Indian camp. The Indians around the camp fire were eating salmon. Marten sat still and watched them. He was hungry and he watched this Indian and then that. Then an Indian threw at him a piece of red salmon. It struck Marten on the breast and the reddish mark is there, even to this day.
