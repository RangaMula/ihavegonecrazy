# The Land Otter

- **Tradition:** Alaska Native peoples (Americas)
- **Source book:** *Myths and Legends of Alaska*
- **Author / collector / translator:** Judson, Katharine Berry (compiler, d. 1929)
- **Original language of this text:** en

Tlingit ( Wrangell )

R AVEN said to Land Otter, “You will live in the water just as well as on land.”

Raven and Land Otter were good friends, so they went halibut-fishing together. Land Otter was a good fisherman. Raven said to Land Otter, “You will always have your house on a point where there are breezes from all sides. Whenever a canoe with people capsizes, you will save the people and make them your friends.” That is how the Land Otter Man was created: because Raven told this to Land Otter.

If people who are taken away by Land Otters are brought back by their friends, they become shamans. It was through the Land Otters that shamans were first known. Shamans, by means of Land Otter spirits, can see each other, even though others cannot.
