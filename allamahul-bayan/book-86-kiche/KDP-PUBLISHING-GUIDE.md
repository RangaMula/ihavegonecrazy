Check especially: the letter names («b'a», «ch'a», «q'a»…); whether to add the glottal stop ( ' ) and the long vowels (aa ee ii oo uu) as their own letters; <i>etz'ab'al</i> (toy) for E; <i>k'im</i> (straw) for K'; <i>laq</i> (plate) for L; <i>mexa</i> (a Spanish loan) for M; <i>nima'</i> (river) for N; <i>saqmo'l</i> for Q and <i>q'ayes</i> for Y, since Q and Y rarely start a word; <i>rax</i> (green) for R, which is a colour word, not a thing; <i>t'uy</i> (small pot) for T'; <i>tzij</i> (word) for TZ; <i>majun</i> for zero; <i>je' · man</i> (yes · no); <i>watz · wanab'</i> (these brother/sister words depend on who is speaking); <i>Kinweta'maj ri K'iche'</i>; and the titles <i>Ri nab'e nuwuj pa K'iche'</i>, <i>We wuj ri' rech</i>, <i>chatz'ib'aj</i>, <i>taq tz'ib'</i>, <i>ajilab'al</i> and <i>Nab'e taq tzij</i>.
