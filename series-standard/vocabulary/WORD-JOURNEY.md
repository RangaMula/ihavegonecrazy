# শব্দের যাত্রা — The 2,000-Word Journey

The vocabulary of every book is told as **one journey**: the reader arrives in a new country, learns to live
there, works and studies, discovers the land, gets to know its people, and finally makes the language their
own. **2,000 words in 6 stages and 20 sections of 100 words.** Each section is a complete step the reader can
finish, see and celebrate.

| Layer | Words | Same in every book? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed sets (F)** | 1,300 (52 sets × 25) | Yes: the same concept slots in every book (Bangla + English fixed) | The words a person needs to live, work and talk |
| **Culture sets (🌏)** | 700 (28 sets × 25) | No: chosen fresh for each language with the native-speaker reviewer | The places, foods, people, festivals, arts, history and wonder words of that world |

Numbers, days and months are taught in Ch. 8–9 and are **not** counted in the 2,000.

---

## 1. How a section gives a sense of accomplishment

Every section follows the same rhythm, so the reader always knows where they are and what they have achieved.

| Step | Page element | What it does for the reader |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Section opener:** a full-page scene + **«এই অংশ শেষে আপনি পারবেন…»** (3 can-do lines) + word counter **«আপনার শব্দভান্ডার: ৩০০ → ৪০০»** | A clear goal before starting, and a visible number that grows |
| 2 | **4 sets of 25 words**, each word with a ☐ tick box | Small, finishable chunks; every tick is a tiny win |
| 3 | **এখনই বলুন — Say it now:** 5 useful sentences built only from words learned so far | Immediate proof that the words *work* |
| 4 | **মিশন — Mission:** one 5-minute real-life task (label 10 things at home, order a meal aloud, greet a neighbour) | Takes the words off the page and into life |
| 5 | **নিজেকে যাচাই — Quick check:** 10 questions, answers in the back | Confirms the learning |
| 6 | **পাসপোর্টে সিল — Stamp your passport:** the reader colours the section's stamp in the **ভাষার পাসপোর্ট** (a 4-page passport inside the front cover, 20 stamps) | A collectable record of progress: 20 stamps, 20 victories |

At the end of each **stage** there is a **milestone page**:

- A celebration line with the new total (e.g. **«অভিনন্দন! আপনি এখন ১,০০০ শব্দ জানেন।»**)
- A **reward reading:** a short scene or story (about 150 words) written **only with words the reader already knows**, so they read a whole text in the new language unaided for the first time, then again at a higher level at every later milestone
- A stage badge in the passport

## 5. Rules for filling the sets

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fixed sets | The concept list (Bangla + English) is written once for the series; each language pack adds target script, Bangla pronunciation and romanization |
| Culture sets | Wonder first, real not stereotyped, breadth across the whole language area, respectful of every faith, at least half usable in real life, every **জানেন কি?** fact sourced |
| Order | Words are introduced so that every **Say it now** sentence and every reward reading uses only words already taught; the build script checks this automatically |
| Verbs and adjectives | Spread through the sections where they are used (buy and sell in the market, hurt and rest in health) rather than kept in separate lists |
| Seed list | `seed_core300.py` holds the first 300 fixed concepts already written; they are redistributed into sections 1–10 when the full fixed list is written |
