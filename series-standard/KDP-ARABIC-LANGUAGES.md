# Language list from the KDP *Introduction to Arabic* series

Source: the author's Google Drive, folder **Amazon KDP**, fetched 1 Oct 2026.
- The edition folders are named «Introduction to Arabic (X)», each with a «Trilingual (X)» twin.
- The original plan is in `Introduction to Arabic/SCHEDULE.md` (Phase 2, 20 languages in priority order).
- Market research is in `KDP-Language-Opportunity-Matrix.csv`.

In that series, X is the **instruction language** of a children's book that teaches **Arabic**.
In the ভাষার দরজা series, the instruction language is always Bangla and X is the **language being taught**.

## 1. Editions that exist on Drive (38 + the English original)

| Group | Languages |
|---|---|
| Original and first wave | English (the source, «Introduction to Arabic for Kids»), **Bangla**, Urdu, French, German, Spanish, Turkish, Indonesian |
| South and Southeast Asia | Assamese, Odia, Nepali, Sinhala, Punjabi (Gurmukhi), Marathi, Telugu, Kannada, Khmer |
| Europe | Czech, Greek, Croatian, Bulgarian, Serbian, Ukrainian, Slovak, Slovenian, Romanian, Belarusian, Macedonian, Latvian, Hungarian, Estonian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Faroese |
| Middle East | Hebrew |
| Africa | Zulu, Xhosa, Kinyarwanda, Shona |

Swahili also has a finished edition status report on Drive, but no Swahili folder was found in the listing.
