# 10-Acre Mustard Plan — Patuakhali coastal land, December sowing

Prepared 20 September 2026. Currency BDT. 10 acres = 4.05 ha = ~30 bigha (33 decimal). 1 maund = 40 kg.

## Constraints of the site and date
- Coastal Patuakhali: late aman harvest, heavy silty-clay soil, residual moisture in December, soil and canal salinity rising from February.
- December sowing is 3-5 weeks past the optimum (mid Oct to mid Nov). Expect 20-30% below optimum yield. Every day after 15 December costs more.
- Therefore: short-duration, salt-tolerant varieties only, sow by 10-15 December, harvest before salinity peaks in March.

## Variety
- First choice: BARI Sarisha-14 (75-80 days, salt tolerance shown in PSTU trials).
- Alternatives: BARI Sarisha-17 (80-85 days, good under late sowing), BINA Sarisha-9 (recommended for saline coast).
- Seed: certified BADC or BARI/BINA seed, 3-3.5 kg per acre broadcast (7-8 kg/ha). Do not use farmer-saved oil-mill seed for 10 acres.

## Calendar
| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| 1-8 Dec | Aman harvest cleared; 2 power-tiller passes at "joe" moisture, ladder to fine tilth; drainage furrows every 8-10 m |
| 5-8 Dec | Basal fertiliser and cow dung incorporated in final ploughing |
| 8-12 Dec (latest 15) | Sow 3-3.5 kg/acre broadcast or 30 cm lines; seed treated (Provax/Vitavax 2.5 g/kg); light ladder to cover |
| 18-22 Dec (10 DAS) | Gap check, aphid scouting starts |
| 25-30 Dec (18-22 DAS) | Thinning to 5-7 cm plant spacing, weeding, top-dress remaining urea, first light irrigation if soil dry |
| 5-15 Jan (28-35 DAS) | Flowering starts; 1% boron foliar spray; place bee boxes |
| 15-25 Jan | Aphid peak: spray at threshold (Imidacloprid 0.5 ml/l or Malathion 2 ml/l), avoid 10am-3pm to protect bees |
| 20-30 Jan (40-50 DAS) | Second irrigation at pod formation if needed; Alternaria watch, Mancozeb or Rovral 2 g/l if spots appear |
| 20 Feb - 5 Mar (75-85 DAS) | Harvest when 80% pods yellow-brown, morning harvest to limit shattering |
| Mar | Sun dry 2-3 days, thresh, dry seed to 8-9% moisture, store in jute bags on dunnage |

## Ways to move toward the good case
1. Register with the Upazila Agriculture Office for the rabi incentive (typically 1 kg seed + 10 kg DAP + 10 kg MoP per bigha), worth roughly ৳30,000 across 30 bigha.
2. Relay sowing: if any aman is still standing on 1 December, broadcast mustard into it 7-10 days before harvest instead of waiting.
3. Bee boxes: 20-30 boxes on 10 acres raise yield 15-20% and give 150-250 kg honey; beekeepers often place boxes free for the honey.
4. Sell after drying and grading rather than wet at the field; ৳200-400 per maund difference.
5. Mustard oil from a local ghani or expeller: 10 acres at 14 maund/acre gives ~5,600 kg seed, ~1,900 litres oil at 34% recovery plus ~3,500 kg oil cake; retail oil at ৳280-320/l roughly doubles gross revenue but needs working capital and a buyer.

## Risks
- Sowing slipping past 15 December: yield falls fast and harvest runs into March salinity and heat.
- Aphids in late-sown mustard are certain, not possible; scout from 10 DAS.
- Irrigation water salinity in February: test canal/pond water, use only if EC under about 2 dS/m.
- Cyclone or unseasonal rain at flowering or harvest.
- Price dip at harvest peak in March; storage capacity lets you sell in April-May.
