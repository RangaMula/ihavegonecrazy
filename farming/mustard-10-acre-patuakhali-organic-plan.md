# 10-Acre Organic Mustard + Honey + Oil Plan — Patuakhali, BARI Sarisha-14, timely sowing

Prepared 20 September 2026. BDT. 10 acres = 4.05 ha = ~30 bigha. 1 maund = 40 kg.
Assumptions set by owner: no chemical fertiliser (organic nutrition only), pesticides allowed, BARI Sarisha-14 sown on time, seed pressed to oil with cake sold at ৳40/kg, own bee boxes for honey.

## Variety and timing
- BARI Sarisha-14: 75-80 days, salt tolerant in PSTU trials, yield potential 1.4-1.6 t/ha (14-16 maund/acre) with full nutrition.
- Timely window: 25 October to 15 November. Requires aman off the field by late October: short-duration aman (BRRI dhan87 or similar) or relay-broadcast mustard into standing aman 7-10 days before harvest.
- Harvest 15 January to 5 February, well before February-March salinity.

## Yield assumption
Full potential 15 maund/acre, minus 15-20% for first-year organic-only nutrition, plus 15-20% from bee pollination and boron foliar. Net: Low 11, Base 14, Good 17 maund/acre.

## Calendar
| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| 15-25 Oct | Aman cleared or relay window; 2 tiller passes, ladder, drainage furrows every 8-10 m |
| 20-28 Oct | Cow dung, poultry litter compost, vermicompost, wood ash, Trichoderma spread and incorporated in final ploughing; biofertiliser seed treatment |
| 28 Oct - 12 Nov | Sow 3-3.5 kg/acre, 30 cm lines preferred; seed treated with Provax 2.5 g/kg plus Azotobacter/PSB slurry |
| 10 DAS | Aphid scouting starts (weekly) |
| 18-22 DAS | Thin to 5-7 cm, weed once, top-dress 200 kg/acre vermicompost along rows, light irrigation if dry |
| 25-35 DAS (late Nov) | Flowering; place 25 bee boxes at field edges; foliar spray 1: seaweed/amino extract (or 1% borax, a mined mineral permitted in organic standards) |
| 40-50 DAS (mid Dec) | Aphid peak: spray at threshold, late afternoon only, bees closed or moved 24 h; foliar spray 2 at pod set; Alternaria check, Mancozeb/Rovral 2 g/l if needed |
| 45-55 DAS | Second irrigation at pod fill if needed (test water salinity first) |
| 75-80 DAS (15 Jan - 5 Feb) | Harvest at 80% pods yellow-brown, morning; sun dry 2-3 days, thresh, dry to 8-9% moisture |
| Feb | Honey final extraction, boxes moved to litchi/black cumin; seed to expeller in lots |

## Working capital needed before first sale
Farm ৳4.56 lakh + honey capital ৳1.88 lakh + honey opex ৳0.66 lakh + pressing/bottling ৳1.19 lakh = about ৳8.3 lakh (own land). Honey cash arrives first (December-January), then oil from February.

## Risks specific to this plan
- Organic N releases slowly; a cold December can show yellowing at 20-30 DAS. Top-dress vermicompost on time and do not skip the foliar sprays.
- Sourcing 50 t of decomposed cow dung and 10 t of poultry litter compost takes weeks; book in September.
- Insecticide kills bees: spray only after 4 pm, close hive entrances that evening, prefer selective products, never spray in full bloom unless aphids exceed threshold.
- Oil marketing is a separate business: line up a buyer or your own retail channel before pressing 2,000 litres.
- Aman must actually be off by late October, or the timely window is lost and yields drop back to the December case.
